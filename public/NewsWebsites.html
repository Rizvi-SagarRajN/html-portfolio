Tips for Evaluating Trustworthy News Sources

  • Check for Transparency: Reliable outlets clearly state their sources and methods.
  • Avoid Sensationalism: Trustworthy news avoids clickbait headlines and exaggerated claims.
  • Cross-Check Information: Verify stories by comparing coverage across multiple reputable sources.
  • Look for Accountability: Reputable outlets issue corrections when errors are made.
